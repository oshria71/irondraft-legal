Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Who we are

Iron Draft (the "Game") is developed and operated by Oshri Amery, Israel ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the Game uses, why, and the choices you have. By playing the Game you acknowledge this Policy. If you do not agree with it, please do not play the Game.

Questions, requests or complaints about privacy: oshria71@gmail.com.

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

We may update this Policy. We will post the new version in the Game before it takes effect, and if you have an account with an email address we will also tell you about material changes by email.

Playing as a guest (no account)

You can play the full Game without an account. As a guest:

  • We do not collect your name, email or any account details.
  • Your coin balance, game settings and privacy choices are stored only on your own device (browser local storage). They are not sent to us. Clearing your browser data or switching device removes them.
  • Our hosting provider receives the technical data every website receives to deliver pages (such as your IP address, browser type and the time of the request). We use it only to deliver and secure the Game.

Signing in (optional)

Signing in is never required to play. It is only needed if you want your records, coins and statistics saved to an account and available on other devices.

If you choose to sign in with Google or Apple, we receive from that provider: a unique account identifier, your email address (with Apple you may choose a private relay address), and, if the provider shares them, your name and profile picture. We use this only to create and secure your account, show your profile, and let you sign in again on other devices. We never receive your Google or Apple password. The provider's own privacy policy applies to its part, and you can revoke our access at any time in your Google or Apple account settings.

With an account we also store your gameplay data: drafts, picks, campaign results, records, coin balance and achievements.

If you play PvP, other players see your ladder name (by default the first name on your Google profile; you can change it in the Game), your saved army, the nations you drew that week, your rating and your win–loss record. Battle results and weekly places are kept after each week ends so prizes can be checked.

Cookies and local storage

The Game uses your browser's local storage, not tracking cookies, for things that are strictly necessary to provide what you ask for: your coins, settings and privacy choices. We ask for your consent before enabling anything non-essential:

  • Necessary (always on): makes the Game work and remembers your choices.
  • Preferences: remembers optional settings such as playback speed.
  • Statistics: anonymous usage analytics.
  • Marketing: advertising, including personalized ads where the law allows.

You can change your choices at any time from Privacy settings in the Game. The Game does not currently respond to browser "Do Not Track" signals; your in-game choices apply instead.

Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information for money. We share it only:

  • with service providers who help us run the Game (hosting, storage, sign-in, analytics and, if enabled, advertising partners), who may use it only to provide their service to us;
  • publicly, if you take part in a leaderboard (display name, results, country);
  • when the law requires it, or to protect the Game, our players or our rights;
  • with a successor if our business is merged, sold or restructured, under this Policy.

How long we keep it

Guest data stays on your device until you clear it. Account data is kept while your account exists and for as long as needed to meet legal obligations, resolve disputes and enforce our agreements. We may keep gameplay statistics in an aggregated or de-identified form that no longer identifies you.

Deleting your account and data

You can ask us to delete your account and all associated data, including what we received from Google or Apple, at any time by emailing oshria71@gmail.com from the address linked to your account (once accounts are available, you will also be able to delete it from your profile in the Game). We will verify the request and complete it within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep certain information.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures, such as HTTPS encryption in transit, to protect your information. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.
